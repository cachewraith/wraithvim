# Code of Conduct

WraithVim follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful; critique ideas, not people;
accept that maintainers may decline contributions; and do not harass anyone,
publicly or privately. Sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks,
trolling and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

The standards apply in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the
contact listed on the project's repository page. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors who violate this code, following
the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
